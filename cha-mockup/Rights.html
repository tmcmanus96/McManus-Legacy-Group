Your Rights & Accessibility

CHA serves everyone fairly. Here's how to ask for an accommodation, get language help, or file a complaint.

Reasonable accommodation

If you have a disability, you can ask for a change to a rule, policy, practice, or your unit. Ask in any way that works for you: by phone, in person, or with our form.

Request formSection 504 Coordinator: [name, phone]

Nondiscrimination & fair housing

CHA does not discriminate based on race, color, religion, sex, national origin, disability, or familial status. If you believe you were treated unfairly, you can tell us or contact HUD.

File a complaint with CHAHUD Fair Housing: 1-800-669-9777

Violence Against Women Act (VAWA)

Survivors of domestic violence, dating violence, sexual assault, or stalking have housing protections, no matter their gender.

VAWA Notice (HUD-5380)Certification (HUD-5382)

Language assistance

Free interpretation and translation are available. Tell any staff member which language you prefer.

Ask for an interpreter: 610-904-1111

Website accessibility statement

This website is designed to meet the Web Content Accessibility Guidelines (WCAG) 2.1 Level AA. You can change the text size at the top of every page, use it with a keyboard or screen reader, and view it on any phone.
